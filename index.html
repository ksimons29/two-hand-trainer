Two-Hand Trainer

Two hands, slowly, with a check

The same exercises as Jazer Lee's levels in step 2, but slow, one level at a time, with a click and a check after every try. Left hand is teal, right hand is orange. The numbers on the keys are your fingers.

Level

Check with

Tap the rhythm with key F (left hand) and key J (right hand), or the two pads below.

  1. 1Watch it twice. Say the count line out loud with the click.
  2. 2Test: one bar to count in, then the level runs twice. Repeat until it says Clean 3 times in a row.
  3. 3Practise on the keys with the click; keys flash green when right, red when wrong. Then 5 bpm faster, or the next level.

    Circle = where the note belongs. Dot = what you played (timing shown 4× bigger so you can see it). Green = on time, amber = a bit off, red = off or wrong key (the letter is the key you hit). A cross is a note that should not be there.

    While you play, check:

    • Shared notes sound as one sound, not two quick ones.
    • The hand that plays less rests on its key in between. It does not lift into the air.
    • Fingers 4 and 5 are as loud as the others.
    • Eyes on the screen, not on your hands.
